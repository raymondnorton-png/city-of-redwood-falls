Program

The City of Redwood Falls provides informational text messages about utility accounts, billing, service requests, permits, inspections, and other city business. This program is not used for advertising or promotions.

Opt-in

You opt in by submitting the form on the text messages page with the consent box checked, or by texting START or YES to 507-616-7400.

Cost and frequency

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

Opt-out and help

Reply STOP to 507-616-7400 to cancel, or use the opt-out form. Reply HELP, use the help form, or call 507-616-7400. Email info@ci.redwood-falls.mn.us. City Hall: Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–4:30 p.m.

Privacy

See the text messaging privacy statement and Website Policies. Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in information are not shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

Service

Available on most U.S. carriers. Consent to text is not required to receive city services.
