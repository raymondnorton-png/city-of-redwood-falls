Text messaging privacy

City of Redwood Falls

Opt in Help Opt out Privacy SMS Terms

This statement is part of the City of Redwood Falls Website Policies and applies to mobile numbers used for city text messages. Privacy of government data is also governed by Minnesota Statutes Chapter 13.

Information collected

When you opt in on the city website or by text, the City collects your mobile number and the name, address, account number, and email you choose to provide. The City also keeps the content of texts sent to and from city numbers and a record of opt-in and opt-out.

Use

The City uses this information to send and receive informational text messages about utility accounts, service requests, permits, inspections, and other city business, and to honor opt-out requests.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Message delivery uses the City’s communications providers. City records may be public under Chapter 13 unless a specific classification applies.

Choices

Reply STOP to 507-616-7400 to opt out, or use the opt-out form. Reply HELP or call 507-616-7400 for help. Message and data rates may apply. Consent to text is not required to receive city services.
